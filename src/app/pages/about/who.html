Our Vision & Purpose

Air Medics Rangers is actively involved in the initiatives of both Air Advocates and KESCA. Together, we aim to implement diverse programs that contribute to environmental preservation.

Back Story

The Air Medics Rangers have been deeply concerned about the deteriorating state of the environment. Witnessing governments and the medical community fail to address the growing environmental challenges, they realized the need for immediate action. Moreover, they noticed how centuries of mismanagement in the construction industry have contributed to the problem. With a vision to reverse the damage and create a sustainable future, the Air Medics Rangers set out to educate adults about environmental preservation before it's too late. They believe in the power of knowledge and want to equip adults with the necessary tools to make a difference.

Introduction

Air Medics Rangers is actively involved in the initiatives of both Air Advocates and KESCA. Together, we aim to implement diverse programs that contribute to environmental preservation.

Utilizing the Volunteer and Coop Systems

We understand the power of collaboration and the impact of collective efforts. To achieve our goals, we seek help from others through volunteer and coop systems. To reach potential volunteers, we post job openings and clearly indicate opportunities for co-op and volunteering on platforms like Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, and Volunteer Ottawa. We believe in engaging talented individuals to support our cause.

Graphic Design Support

As we expand our reach, we need graphic design assistance to create impactful visuals that resonate with our audience. We are actively looking for creative minds who can contribute to designing new logos for initiatives like Dead Beat Patrol and Slum Lord Patrol, presenting them in a way that resembles official logos like those of police, fire, and health vehicles. Additionally, we aim to revamp our websites, design business stationery, and create fundraising materials like activity book pamphlets and health and wellness pamphlets.

Air Advocates and KESCA Walking Tours

To engage the community and raise awareness, we plan to organize Air Advocates and KESCA walking tours. These tours will allow participants to experience first-hand the impact of environmental issues and promote the importance of a cleaner environment. To facilitate user engagement, we are working on coding KLM files that will enable users to register offenders by posting their addresses for initiatives like Slum Lord Patrol and Dead Beat Patrol.

Building a Strong Team

To ensure the smooth operation of our initiatives, we need a well-coordinated team. Our team will consist of graphic designers, web developers, social network influencers, volunteers, and coop students. Together, we can leverage our diverse skills and expertise to make a lasting impact.

Collaborating with Influencers

To expand our reach and influence, we plan to collaborate with existing influencers, including social network personalities, artists, game developers, writers, and actors. By forming partnerships with these influencers and utilizing platforms like Facebook groups, we can effectively communicate our message and drive engagement with our puppets.

Ensuring Anonymity and Security

We recognize the importance of data security and anonymity in our work. To protect our operations, we plan to set up computers downstairs and use VPN (Virtual Private Network) to ensure our communications and identities remain secure.

Collaborating with Aboriginal Groups

As we advocate for a cleaner future, we believe in inclusive collaboration. To promote diversity and collaboration, we aim to reach out to Aboriginal groups and engage them in initiatives like the Strong Point Adventure Park, Living Museum, and Housing Supply Challenge. By working together, we can create a more sustainable and inclusive environment.

Our Approach

At Air Medics Rangers, we believe in addressing environmental problems head-on, without masking the issues or resorting to temporary solutions. Our goal is to instill family values and a strong code of ethics that future generations can emulate. We are an inclusive platform, promoting equality and inclusivity regardless of physical disability, race, or religion. Everyone has a unique strong point, and by coming together, we can create a better future for our environment. Features of this platform:

  • Public Awareness Videos: We produce informative and engaging videos that raise awareness about environmental challenges and inspire action.
  • The Rant Puppet News: Our puppet news segment humorously delivers important messages about environmental issues using a variety of quirky puppets.
  • Stop Motion Animations Contests: We encourage creativity through stop-motion animation contests that tackle environmental themes.
  • Fundraising Opportunities: Through various fundraising initiatives, we support schools and causes that align with our mission of cleaning the environment.
  • Collaboration with Schools, Churches & Social Network Influencers: We partner with educational institutions, religious organizations, and social media influencers to expand our reach and impact.
  • Friendly Challenges: Our platform hosts a range of friendly challenges both online and locally, encouraging participation from artists, fashion designers, musicians, and more, all dedicated to environmental causes.
